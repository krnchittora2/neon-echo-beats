# Neon Echo

**A synthwave rhythm + memory arcade game.**

### ▶ [Play now](https://krnchittora2.github.io/neon-echo-beats/)

Runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Nothing to install.

---

## About

Neon pads light up in a pulsing sequence, each with its own synth note. Repeat the pattern, and it grows longer and faster every round. Watch out for **glitch rounds**: the board can rotate, mirror, invert colors, or fire fake pulses to throw you off.

How long can you keep the signal alive?

## How to Play

1. Pick your **difficulty** and **pad layout** (2×2 or 2×3), then hit **START**.
2. **Watch** the pads light up.
3. **Repeat** the sequence in the same order.
4. Each correct round adds more steps. One wrong pad or running out of time ends the game.

Chain correct hits to build your **combo** and score multiplier.

### Controls

| Action | Mouse / Touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Hit a pad | Click / tap the pad | `1` – `6` |
| Pause / resume | `II` button | `Space` |
| Back to menu | Quit to Menu | `Esc` |

### Difficulty

| | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tempo | Relaxed | Faster | Frantic |
| Glitch rounds | Every 5th | Every 3rd | Every 2nd |
| Score multiplier | ×1 | ×1.5 | ×2.2 |

### Ranks

Signal Rookie → Signal Scout → Neon Runner → Neon Champion → **Cyber Phantom**

## Accessibility

- **Sound:** on or off.
- **Reduced Motion:** turns off animations and particle bursts.
- **Colorblind mode:** adds a unique symbol to each pad.

Your settings and high score are saved in your browser.
